How Word Stream Works

No fixed curriculum. No pressure. You decide what you learn and how fast you progress.

Step 01
Add Your Words

Create your own personal dictionary. Add words manually or import them from files in your target languages.

Step 02
Generate Smart Quizzes

Word Stream builds quizzes from your words, focusing more on what you struggle with and less on what’s easy.

Step 03
Train Your Memory

Words come back over time based on their strength, so you move them from “I think I know this” to “I won’t forget this”.

Step 04
Review & Adjust

Check your stats, tweak difficulty, and curate your lists. You stay fully in control of how and what you learn.

Designed for Long-Term Learning

Word Stream is not about quick wins. It’s built for real progress. Every word you add becomes part of a growing system that evolves with you.

Whether you’re learning one language or five, preparing for exams, traveling, or sharpening professional vocabulary — your learning flow stays organized, measurable, and effective.

You control the data. You control the pace. You control the results.
