Word Learning App

A Python & Django-based web application designed to help users expand their vocabulary through custom word lists and auto-generated quizzes.

---

Key Features
- Custom Vocabularies: Build, edit, and organize your personal word collections.
- Auto-Generated Quizzes: Automatically create practice quizzes based on your saved vocabulary.
- User Authentication: Secure sign-up, login, and personal account management.

---

Quick Testing Instructions
To test the quiz features:
1. Create an account (or log into your account).
2. Add at least 10 words to your vocabulary (the quiz generator requires a minimum of 10 entries to generate a quiz session).
3. Navigate to the Quiz section to test your knowledge.
